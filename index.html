      Settings

      Auto-lock

      The vault locks itself and wipes decrypted data from memory after this much inactivity.

      Update password

      Change your master password, and your username if you want. Your recovery key keeps working.

      Password recovery

      Backup

      The vault lives only in this browser. Clearing site data or switching devices loses it unless you keep a backup. Backups stay encrypted and need your username and password to open.

      How your data is protected

      Encryption
      AES-256-GCM, fresh random IV on every save
      Key
      PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds, from your username and password
      Storage
      This browser only, as ciphertext. Nothing is sent to a server.
      Recovery
      A random 256-bit recovery key can also unlock the vault. Only you and your inbox ever see it.
      Wrong guesses
      Lockout delay grows after 3 failed attempts

      Delete vault

      Permanently erases everything stored on this device.
